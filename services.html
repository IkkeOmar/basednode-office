§ 01 — What I build

Seven systems. Here is what goes into each one.

Each service below starts the same way: I look at how the work is actually done, and build the system that takes over. Most companies need two of them. Here is what you get, in plain terms, before we talk about price.

7 services Fixed price from 4,000 DKK Delivered in 2–8 weeks
§ 01.1 — The seven

What I build, in detail.

Seven things, all of them the same idea: take the repetitive half of somebody's week off them. You do not need all seven. Most people start with one and come back for the next.

§ 01

AI agents

from 8,500 DKK

One job, however narrow — learned properly, then done on its own

  • One agent per task: it reads your own documents and writes the answer, not a generic guess.
  • Retrieval over your files — contracts, price lists, past mails — with the source shown for every answer.
  • Fine-tuned on your own examples where the wording matters: sales replies, order confirmations.
  • Runs on your own server, calling the OpenAI or Anthropic API with your key, never mine.
  • Handover is the code, the prompts, an evaluation set and a runbook you can follow yourself.
At handover

The agent runs on your machine, not on mine. You get the prompts, the evaluation set and the rules for when it must ask a human instead of guessing. If it drifts, you can see where.

§ 02

Workflow automation

from 12,000 DKK

Processes you already run, tidied up so nobody loses a day to Excel

  • I map the process as it actually runs, including the workarounds nobody has written down.
  • Built in n8n, so the flow stays visible and editable without touching code.
  • Connects to your ERP, CRM, mail and shared drive, usually through existing APIs.
  • Replaces the copy-paste between two systems and the Excel file in the middle of it.
  • Alerts go to the person who owns the task, so nothing fails quietly in a queue.
At handover

The flows live in n8n, so you can open one and read it. Nothing runs that you cannot open. The manual step it replaced is usually deleted the same week we finish.

§ 03

Data pipelines

from 9,000 DKK

Data from five systems, cleaned up and joined into one

  • Sources pulled on a schedule: SQL database, CSV drop, REST API, ERP export.
  • Cleaned and joined in Postgres, with every transformation written down as code.
  • A stable table per source, so nothing downstream has to parse raw files a second time.
  • Loads into your BI tool or warehouse without the manual export on the first of the month.
  • You get the schema, the run log and a restart button for the day something breaks.
At handover

The schema, the SQL and a runbook. If someone else has to take it over, they can read how it works and change it without calling me.

§ 04

Cost work

from 4,000 DKK

Same answer, smaller bill for the model calls

  • Prompts and context trimmed until the answer is the same length as it was.
  • Caching for the repeated questions that were being paid for every single time.
  • Smaller model for the easy steps, larger one only where it changes the answer.
  • Token usage logged per call, so the saving shows up as a number and not a feeling.
  • On one project the monthly bill went from €12,000 to under €150.
At handover

A before-and-after number on your own usage, plus the changes that produced it. You keep the cheaper setup whether or not we work together again.

§ 05

Dashboards and internal tools

from 15,000 DKK

One screen with the numbers, askable in plain Danish

  • One screen with the numbers you actually check, and nothing else.
  • Ask a question in Danish and get an answer with the table behind it.
  • Built on FastAPI with Postgres behind it — the same stack I run in production.
  • Replaces the weekly report somebody builds by hand and sends out on Friday.
  • Access rights per role, so people see their own numbers and not everyone else's.
At handover

The app, the database and a set of accounts with the right access. You can add a question the dashboard answers without me.

§ 06

Marketing automation

from 7,000 DKK

Every lead followed up, without a spreadsheet in sight

  • Leads from your forms, site and mail land in one place — no spreadsheet in between.
  • Sequences written for your actual offer, with the timing you would have used yourself.
  • Synced with your CRM, so the status of every lead is visible without asking anyone.
  • Every send is logged: who received what, when, and what happened next.
  • Sequences stop on a reply, so no lead is contacted twice by accident.
At handover

The sequences, the templates and the CRM fields. Everything is editable, and the log shows what each lead answered.

§ 05 — Questions

The five questions everyone asks first.

How long does a project take?

Two to eight weeks, depending on scope. The first working version is usually live inside three weeks, because I would rather show you something running than describe it.

Do I have to replace the systems I already use?

Usually not. Most of the work is connecting what you already have — your ERP, your CRM, your mail — and removing the manual step in between. Nothing gets ripped out unless you want it to.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, and I will not guess from a website form. After the first call you get a written scope and a fixed price, so you can compare it against doing nothing.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do, from day one. Everything is delivered in your own accounts, with documentation written for someone who has not seen the code before.

Do you work with companies outside Denmark?

Yes. The work is remote, and I have delivered for teams in Denmark, Germany and Sweden. Everything is in English and Danish, so nothing gets lost in translation.
