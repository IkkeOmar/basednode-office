§ 03 — What happens next

Three steps before anything is built.

Step 01

You write, I reply

I read the message and reply within one working day. If it is clear what you want built, I send a suggestion for scope and price straight away. Otherwise I ask one question.

Step 02

A thirty-minute call

We look at your week and your systems. I tell you what can be automated, what cannot, and how long it takes. If I am not the right person for it, I say so on that call.

Step 03

Written scope and fixed price

You get a written description of exactly what the system does, and a fixed price. Only once you have approved it do I start. If the scope changes along the way, the price changes with it, in writing.

§ 04 — Start

Say what is slow in your week.

Starting costs nothing and commits you to nothing. I reply within one working day, and if I am not the right person for it, I tell you who is.
