About

It is just me. That is the point.

BasedNode is me, Omar, a civil engineer in physics and nanotechnology from DTU. I build software for companies that have more of the same work every day than they have time for. I have taught maths and programming since 2019 and worked with AI at GXU since 2025. And I am the one writing the code — not an agency with people you never meet.

Copenhagen · CVR 43750194 DTU · Physics & Nanotechnology Languages: Danish and English
§ 01 — Why it is me

I can do it cheaply, because making it expensive is not my life's work.

There is a reason most small companies have not automated anything: hiring a developer is expensive, and it is hard to know whether it is worth it. I think most of the processes running inside a small company are easy to automate. They have simply never been built, because nobody had the time to go in and find out how the work actually gets done. That is what I go in and do. And because it is me rather than an agency, it costs less, and you talk to the person writing the code.

Wedge 01 / 03

You talk to me, the whole way.

There is no account manager, no sales department and no handover to a junior. The person on the call is the person building it, and the person answering when the system does something unexpected.

No middleman · One person · Every step

Wedge 02 / 03

I know the trade, not just the software.

I have used the systems I build against. I know what a Danish invoice looks like, what account 5700 is, and why an accountant asks for a receipt. That is why the systems fit into what you already have instead of requiring you to change everything.

Bookkeeping · Finance · Teaching · Operations

Wedge 03 / 03

I do not take work I cannot do.

Sometimes a problem needs a different kind of specialist. When that is the case I say so on the first call, and I tell you who can help. That is why I spend half an hour on the first call for free.

Free 30 minutes · Honest answer · Written scope

§ 02 — Background

Teaching, industry and software since 2019.

2019

Teaching maths and programming

Through MentorDanmark I have taught secondary school students maths and programming. It taught me to explain a difficult subject to someone who is not an expert without sounding condescending. I use that ability in every single project.

2020

Software built in real operating environments

I have built and run systems that are live every day, with real data and real consequences. It taught me the things no course teaches you: that a system has to survive a Tuesday when 400 invoices arrive at once, and that someone has to be able to take it over.

2025

AI in production at GXU

AI on the workplace, integrated into processes that were already running, producing output people can actually use. Not demos — systems in operation.

Today

BasedNode

My own company, built around one idea: that most small companies have more repetitive work than they can afford to have automated. I go in, learn the work, and build the system.
